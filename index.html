Project 2 · Term 2 & 4

RCV Spy Car

ESP32-CAM WiFi Robot

For this project, you must build a physical remote‑controlled vehicle with a camera (FPV).
Your mechatronics solution will use:
• an ESP32‑CAM microcontroller,
• a motor driver (e.g., L298/L298N) and DC motors,
• and you will write code so the car can be driven from a web page while streaming live video to a browser on the same network.
You will:
• Use a microcontroller (ESP32‑CAM) with onboard camera.
• Include working inputs (web‑page controls/sensors) and outputs (DC motors via motor controller).
• Complete coding challenges to control forward, reverse, left, right, stop and maintain a live video stream.
• Document your work as digital logbook entries on Class OneNote.
• Create a 3‑minute video demonstrating your project’s functionality.

What is Mechatronics?

Mechatronics combines 3D design, electronics and code to build machines that sense, think and move. In Term 1 you'll model in 3D, wire up sensors and outputs, and learn to code an Arduino Uno — recording each step in your digital logbook. In Term 2 you'll bring it all together: program, build and test your own robotic vehicle with working sensors and motors.
